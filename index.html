This policy covers the 1.2.7 no-ads offline release (build 006) and version 1.2.8 with optional Yandex rewarded ads. Local gameplay and the three free home rewards remain available without watching ads. Advertising details below apply to version 1.2.8; installing or continuing to use the earlier offline build does not enable ads in that build.

本政策涵盖1.2.7无广告离线发布版（006）及提供自选Yandex激励广告的1.2.8版本。本地玩法和三项首页免费奖励均不要求观看广告。下文广告处理说明适用于1.2.8；安装或继续使用旧离线包，不会使旧包自动启用广告。

The RuStore editions are operated by Wire Technology Limited: code 67 has optional Yandex ads; the earlier code 63 candidate has no ads. Read the RuStore privacy notice; the sections below do not describe that edition.

RuStore 渠道运营者为 Wire Technology Limited：code67 提供可选 Yandex 广告，早期 code63 候选包无广告。请阅读RuStore 版本隐私说明；本页下文不适用于该版本。

About the versions / 关于各版本

This policy describes Idle Tower Defense for Android, package com.Ion.IdleTowerDefense. The operator is 李至超 (Li Zhichao). Contact 1830819660@qq.com for support or privacy requests.

本政策说明Android游戏Idle Tower Defense（包名com.Ion.IdleTowerDefense）的信息处理方式。运营者为李至超（Li Zhichao）。支持及隐私请求请联系1830819660@qq.com。

The 1.2.7 no-ads offline release, identified as build 006, does not provide ads and has no Android internet-access permission. Version 1.2.8 keeps local gameplay and adds a separate optional rewarded ad. The availability of a version in a store may vary; this policy describes each version’s behavior.

1.2.7无广告离线发布版（006）不提供广告，也没有Android互联网访问权限。1.2.8保留本地玩法，并增加独立的可选激励广告。不同商店版本的可用时间可能不同，本政策分别说明各版本的行为。

The three original home rewards remain free. Coins and diamonds retain their original accumulation rules and a 10-minute interval between claims; that interval does not create stock. The separate fixed 20-diamond free reward keeps its 30-minute countdown. The optional ad reward is additional. In-app purchase entries remain hidden, and version 1.2.8 does not offer purchases for real money.

原有三项首页奖励继续免费。金币和钻石保留原积累规则及10分钟领取间隔；领取间隔不会自动创造库存。独立的固定20钻免费奖励保留30分钟倒计时。广告奖励是另外增加的可选奖励。内购入口继续隐藏，1.2.8不提供真实付费购买。

Information on your device / 设备上的信息

The game stores your progress, earned coins and diamonds, upgrades, cards, unlocked content, display name, settings, reward stock and claim times on your device. Local name editing remains available. These records allow you to resume play without a game cloud account.

游戏在设备上保存进度、已获得的金币和钻石、升级、卡牌、解锁内容、显示名、设置、奖励库存和领取时间。本地改名继续保留。这些记录使你无需游戏云账号就能继续游玩。

Local timing uses device time, elapsed time and a boot counter for reward intervals and offline accumulation. It does not add a time server. Updates preserve existing saves. Clearing Android app data or uninstalling can erase local progress; neither action deletes records already held by an online provider.

本地计时使用设备时间、经过时长及开机计数，处理奖励间隔和离线积累，没有增加计时服务器。更新保留现有存档。清除Android应用数据或卸载可能删除本地进度；这些操作都不会删除在线服务方已经持有的记录。

Version 1.2.8 reads local age and advertising-choice records, including when a choice was recorded, to decide whether advertising may start. It also keeps local request, error and reward-transaction records to prevent duplicate requests or rewards and to diagnose failures. The game adds no automatic upload of these host records or its local game saves. Advertising SDK processing is described separately below.

1.2.8会读取本地年龄及广告选择记录，包括记录时间，用于决定能否启动广告；还保存本地请求、错误和奖励事务记录，以防止重复请求、重复发奖并排查失败。游戏没有新增对这些宿主记录或本地游戏存档的自动上传。广告SDK自身的处理在下文另行说明。

Earlier age preferences, service sign-in caches and local technical information may remain on the device. Retaining a record does not by itself mean the related service is running.

旧年龄偏好、服务登录缓存及本地技术信息可能仍保存在设备上。保留记录本身不表示相关服务正在运行。

Optional advertising / 可选广告

Version 1.2.8 uses the native Yandex Mobile Ads 8.3.0 rewarded-ad interface and its AppMetrica dependencies. Version 1.2.8 does not route the ad request through TopOn mediation, and does not start the previous TopOn/ADX, Tramini or Start.io ad paths. Merely retaining those SDK files does not enable them.

1.2.8使用Yandex Mobile Ads 8.3.0原生激励广告接口及其AppMetrica依赖。1.2.8不经过TopOn聚合，也不启动旧TopOn/ADX、Tramini或Start.io广告路径。仅保留这些SDK文件并不表示启用其功能。

An eligible user must choose to load an ad. The game then applies the saved privacy choice before explicitly initializing and requesting Yandex ads. It does not preload ads at startup or retry automatically. A loaded ad requires a separate choice to watch. Declining to use an ad leaves the free rewards and basic game available.

符合条件的用户需要主动选择加载广告。游戏会先应用保存的隐私选择，再明确初始化并请求Yandex广告；不会在启动时预加载，也不会自动重试。加载完成后仍需另行选择观看。不使用广告不影响免费奖励或基础游戏。

Version 1.2.8 has Android internet-access, network-state and advertising-ID permissions. Subject to the device settings, permissions and your choice, the SDK may use advertising identifiers to serve and measure ads. Network requests also disclose transmission information such as an IP address to the services receiving them. See Yandex Privacy Policy for Yandex’s practices.

1.2.8具有Android互联网访问、网络状态和广告标识权限。在设备设置、权限和你的选择允许时，SDK可使用广告标识提供和衡量广告。接收网络请求的服务也会获得IP地址等传输信息。Yandex的信息处理请参见Yandex隐私政策。

SDK location tracking is disabled, and version 1.2.8 does not request Android precise or coarse location permission. This does not prevent a server from estimating a general region from network information. App-ad analytics reporting is also disabled; this setting is not a switch that disables every ad request, measurement or SDK processing operation. The game does not activate a separate AppMetrica analytics account.

SDK定位追踪已关闭，1.2.8不申请Android精确或粗略定位权限；这并不阻止服务器根据网络信息估计大致地区。应用广告分析上报接口也已关闭，但这个设置不是关闭所有广告请求、测量或SDK处理的总开关。游戏没有另行激活独立的AppMetrica分析账号。

Automatic advertising initialization is disabled in the Android configuration. SDK support components can still be loaded by Android; their presence is not evidence that all advertising is active or that no technical processing ever occurs before an ad request. The game’s age and choice checks control its explicit initialization and loading calls.

Android配置已关闭广告自动初始化。Android仍可加载SDK支持组件；这些组件的存在不能证明所有广告都在运行，也不能据此保证广告请求前完全不存在技术处理。游戏的年龄和选择检查控制宿主发起的明确初始化和加载调用。

Your advertising choice / 你的广告选择

Version 1.2.8 does not change a previous choice to personalized advertising automatically. It allows the ad flow only with a valid adult declaration and an explicit personalized or non-personalized choice. A missing, invalid or blocked choice prevents advertising initialization and requests. Non-personalized does not mean that an ad request sends no information.

1.2.8不会把原有选择自动改为个性化。只有有效的成年年龄声明，以及明确的个性化或非个性化选择，才允许进入广告流程。缺失、无效或已阻断的选择会阻止广告初始化和请求。非个性化不等于广告请求不发送任何信息。

Version 1.2.8 provides privacy settings through the game’s battle menu: MENU → Privacy settings. The screen shows the saved age and advertising choice. You can save your actual age, choose personalized or non-personalized ads, or choose “Use no ads / withdraw ad choice”. Merely opening the screen, or saving an age, does not consent to advertising or load an ad.

1.2.8在游戏战斗菜单中提供MENU → 隐私设置。页面显示已保存的年龄及广告选择；你可以保存实际年龄，选择个性化或非个性化广告，或选择“不使用广告／撤回广告选择”。仅打开页面或保存年龄，不表示同意广告，也不会加载广告。

An earlier advertising choice is not automatically treated as confirmation of the Yandex disclosure. After reading the explanation, an adult can explicitly confirm the existing choice or select a different one. The game records the confirmation for that age and choice. Changing the saved age invalidates the earlier confirmation; ads remain blocked until an eligible user explicitly confirms a choice again.

旧广告选择不会自动被视为已确认Yandex说明。阅读说明后，成年用户可以明确确认保留原选择，或选择另一种方式。游戏会为对应的年龄及选择记录确认。修改保存的年龄会使旧确认失效；在符合条件的用户再次明确确认选择前，广告保持阻断。

A successfully saved change cancels a game ad flow that has not begun presentation. Later game-initiated requests follow the new choice and do not start automatically. Withdrawal blocks future game-initiated initialization and requests. While an ad is already presenting, the settings ask you to return using the ad’s own close controls before making a change, so an earned reward is not lost. If saving fails, the screen reports the failure and blocks ads; do not treat that message as confirmation of a durable preference change.

更改成功保存后，游戏会取消尚未开始展示的广告流程。之后由游戏发起的请求按新选择处理，不会自动加载。撤回会阻止之后由游戏发起的初始化和请求。广告已开始展示时，设置页会要求先使用广告自身关闭方式返回，再修改设置，以免丢失已经取得的奖励。若保存失败，页面会提示失败并阻断广告；该提示不代表选择已经成功持久保存。

Changing a local choice cannot recall information already sent or erase records held by a provider. Closing a screen or stopping new game-initiated requests is not proof that every task already running inside an SDK has stopped.

改变本地选择不能撤回已发送的信息，也不能删除服务方持有的记录。关闭页面或停止游戏发起的新请求，并不能证明SDK内部已经运行的所有任务都已停止。

Game services and earlier versions / 游戏服务与旧版本

The original Unity Authentication, Cloud Save, Cloud Code and Analytics paths, PlayFab online leaderboards, automatic cloud sign-in, cloud time, cloud saves and save transfer remain disabled in version 1.2.8. Transfer and online-leaderboard entries remain hidden. Local play, saving and name editing remain available. Restoring internet permission for optional ads does not intentionally restore those game-cloud paths.

1.2.8继续停用原Unity Authentication、Cloud Save、Cloud Code及Analytics路径、PlayFab在线排行榜、自动云登录、云时间、云存档和存档转移。转移及在线排行榜入口继续隐藏；本地游玩、保存和改名继续保留。为可选广告恢复联网权限，不表示恢复这些游戏云服务路径。

The package retains the Unity game engine and historical SDK components. A library name or configuration file alone is not evidence of collection or upload. This policy distinguishes retained code from the Yandex advertising path that can actually be initialized after the client checks.

安装包保留Unity游戏引擎及历史SDK组件。仅有库名称或配置文件，不足以证明正在收集或上传。本政策区分残留代码与通过客户端检查后可以实际初始化的Yandex广告路径。

Earlier versions contained Unity authentication and cloud saving, PlayFab ranking and advertising code. The earlier transfer path could include a transfer username and password in cloud-save data; this does not show that the records were public. Some earlier requests may already have been received or stored. This update does not recall those requests or delete historical cloud data.

旧版本包含Unity认证和云存档、PlayFab排行及广告代码。旧存档转移路径可能把转移用户名和密码包含在云存档数据中；这不能证明这些记录是公开的。部分旧请求可能已被接收或保存。本次更新不会撤回这些请求，也不会删除历史云数据。

The operator has not verified every original service project’s access controls, storage region, retention period or current data holdings, and does not claim management rights over projects outside their control. Questions about earlier provider-held records require the relevant authorized party and its process.

运营者尚未核实每个原服务项目的访问控制、存储地域、保存期限或当前持有的数据，也不声称拥有其无权控制的项目的管理权限。关于旧服务方记录的问题，需要由相应有权限的责任方依其流程处理。

Children and families / 儿童与家庭

The game is available to a mixed audience, including children. Basic gameplay and the three free rewards do not require advertising. The no-ads offline version provides no ads for any age.

本游戏面向包含儿童的混合受众。基础玩法和三项免费奖励都不要求使用广告。无广告离线版本对所有年龄均不提供广告。

In version 1.2.8, unknown ages and users under 18 do not initialize or request advertising. Age 18 is this client’s technical gate, not a statement that every jurisdiction defines childhood or advertising consent in the same way. The game does not treat everyone as an adult or everyone as a child.

1.2.8对年龄未知及未满18岁的用户不初始化、不请求广告。18岁是本客户端的技术门禁，不表示各地区对儿童或广告同意年龄都采用相同定义。游戏不会把所有人统一标为成人或儿童。

Version 1.2.8 adds no identity-based age verification or parental-authorization system. A saved advertising preference is not proof of parental authorization. A parent or guardian may contact the operator about information concerning a child, including possible historical records. Installing an update does not itself erase those records.

1.2.8没有新增基于身份证明的年龄核验或监护人授权系统。保存的广告偏好不是监护人授权的证明。家长或监护人可以就孩子的信息联系运营者，包括可能存在的历史记录；安装更新本身不会抹除这些记录。
