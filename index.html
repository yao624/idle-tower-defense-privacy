1. Operator and contact

The proposed operator is 李至超 (Li Zhichao). For support or questions about this draft, contact 1830819660@qq.com.

This policy is intended to cover Idle Tower Defense for Android, package com.Ion.IdleTowerDefense, including the free version with optional rewarded advertising. This version does not offer purchases for real money. Spending coins or diamonds earned within the game is part of gameplay, not a payment transaction.

2. Information stored on your device

The game saves progress, earned coins and diamonds, upgrades, cards, unlocked content, display name, settings and reward timing on your device. It also stores your advertising choice, when that choice was made, and information needed to keep a withdrawn choice blocked after restart. These records let the game resume progress and respect your advertising preference.

The game and its SDKs may also keep technical caches and local diagnostic information. Local records generally remain until the app updates or removes them, or you remove the app's data through Android. Clearing data or uninstalling can remove progress. Removing local information does not itself delete information already held by an online service.

3. Optional rewarded advertising

The app includes Start.io Android SDK 5.4.0, provided by Start.io Inc. and its affiliates. Ads are optional. You can keep playing without enabling or watching them. The app asks you to choose a preference and explicitly enable rewarded ads before its advertising bridge starts ad requests. It does not enable ads merely because you opened the privacy page.

Ad delivery involves network communication. Depending on the applicable configuration and information available, Start.io may process IP/network information, device and app information, language or region settings, ad requests and interactions, and technical diagnostics to deliver, measure and protect its services. Non-personalized advertising does not mean that no information is processed. The current child-directed configuration restricts advertising-identifier access; this draft does not claim that every possible network field has been independently verified.

Start.io explains its processing, partners, transfers, retention and rights procedures in its End User Privacy Policy. You can contact its privacy team at privacy@start.io. The exact recipient chain and remote retention applicable to this app have not been independently confirmed; they must not be represented as a fixed period or a guarantee of local-only processing.

4. Your choices and withdrawal

To reopen the controls, enter a battle, open the three-line MENU in the upper-right corner, and select Privacy Settings. In release003 this screen links to the ad provider's policy. This app-specific policy is not yet hosted or linked in the game. The screen lets you change your preference, withdraw your choice, or return to the game without enabling ads.

Personalized and non-personalized are preference choices. Choosing a preference does not identify you as an adult or override child-directed restrictions. This build does not ask for or infer a numerical age.

Selecting Withdraw Ad Choice stops new rewarded-ad requests and use of loaded ads through the game's advertising bridge. The withdrawn choice is saved and remains blocked after a cold start. Previously earned rewards and game progress are retained. Withdrawal does not cancel a request already sent, reverse a completed reward, or prove that the provider has deleted previously received information. Contact the operator and, where relevant, Start.io to request information or deletion. Your advertising choice is not a control for separate game cloud or ranking functions.

5. Children and families

The planned audience includes children and adults. The current build declares a mixed audience and applies child-directed treatment to all users rather than assuming an unknown user is an adult. A personalization selection is not verification of age or parental authorization.

The operator must confirm the applicable conditions for this audience and any required parental-consent process before publication. No verified parental-consent process has been established by this draft. A parent or guardian can contact the operator about a child's information. Until these conditions are resolved, the draft must not be presented as certification that the app satisfies all children's privacy requirements.

6. Original online game features — verification pending

The retained game includes connections for Unity Gaming Services authentication, save transfer and time-related functions, and Microsoft PlayFab ranking features. The operator has not introduced a new account server or payment server in this release candidate. That does not mean the app has no online services or that all records stay on the device.

The actual enabled remote flows, information transmitted, original service-account controller, retention and deletion arrangements still need confirmation. Game display names, generated player identifiers, scores and saved progress are relevant categories to investigate. The current review has not established successful transmission of all these categories. This section must be finalized from the real service arrangements before publication; it is not a statement that every retained SDK is actively collecting data.

7. Android permissions and declared capabilities

The release003 manifest declares Internet access and network-state access for online game and advertising connections. It also declares advertising-ID and Android AdServices ID, attribution and Topics permissions. A permission declaration describes a capability; it is not evidence that each API is called or that data is transmitted. The child-directed configuration described above restricts advertising-ID access and must remain effective independently of a personalization preference.

Other retained declarations include wake lock, foreground service, vibration, boot completion, install-referrer binding and an app-scoped non-exported receiver permission. These do not by themselves establish that every related background feature is active. This review has not confirmed all retained SDK background behavior.

The reviewed manifest does not request camera, microphone, contacts, precise or approximate location, SMS-reading or phone-number permissions. Package-visibility queries for browsers, a calendar insertion action, SMS and dialer handlers are not permissions to read those records. Existing game and SDK code is not assumed to be active solely because it remains in the package.

8. Support, external links and requests

If you email support, the operator will receive your email address and the information you choose to send so that your request can be handled. Do not send payment credentials, passwords or unnecessary identifying information. The support retention process still needs to be established before this draft becomes effective.

The game retains a More TD App link that opens another application's store listing. Opening an external link places you under the relevant site's or app's own information practices. This does not initiate an in-app purchase in this game.

You can ask the operator about access, correction or deletion and other rights available under the law that applies to you. The operator must confirm how requests involving the original online-service accounts will be fulfilled. A withdrawal button is not a substitute for that process.
