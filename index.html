About this version / 关于本版本

This policy describes Idle Tower Defense for Android, package com.Ion.IdleTowerDefense. The operator is 李至超 (Li Zhichao). Contact 1830819660@qq.com for support or privacy requests.

本政策说明 Android 游戏 Idle Tower Defense（包名 com.Ion.IdleTowerDefense）的信息处理方式。运营者为李至超（Li Zhichao）。支持及隐私请求请联系 1830819660@qq.com。

Information on your device / 设备上的信息

The game keeps your progress, earned coins and diamonds, upgrades, cards, unlocked content, display name, settings, reward stock and claim timing on your device. You can still change your name locally. These records let you resume your game. Reward timing and offline stock accumulation use local time records rather than a cloud clock.

The local timing system records device time, elapsed time and a boot counter to keep timing state. On first use, the new offline clock establishes a starting point; it does not invent past elapsed time when there is no reliable local record. This update adds no timing server or account.

Updating preserves your existing save. Records from earlier versions, such as age and advertising preferences or service sign-in caches, may remain on the device. Keeping an old record does not itself mean the related service is running. The game engine may also keep local technical or error information.

Local records change as you play. Clearing Android app data or uninstalling can remove your progress; do not use those actions as a way to delete records held by an online service.

游戏在设备上保存进度、已获得的金币和钻石、升级、卡牌、解锁内容、显示名、设置、奖励库存和领取时间，让你继续原来的游戏。本地改名仍可使用。奖励计时及离线库存累计使用本地时间记录，不再依赖云端时间。

本地计时会记录设备时间、经过时长和开机计数，以保存计时状态。新的离线时钟首次启用时只建立本地起点；没有可靠记录的过去时长不会凭空补算。本次没有新增计时服务器或账号。

更新会保留现有存档。旧版留下的年龄、广告偏好或服务登录缓存等记录可能仍在设备上；保留记录本身不表示相关服务仍在运行。游戏引擎也可能保留本地技术或错误信息。

本地记录随游戏使用而更新。通过 Android 清除应用数据或卸载可能导致进度丢失；这不是删除在线服务记录的方法。

Online features and earlier versions / 联网功能与旧版本

This version has removed Android internet-access and network/Wi-Fi-state permissions. The game cannot directly connect to the internet. Automatic cloud sign-in, cloud time and saving, save transfer, online leaderboards, and the original services’ analytics/startup paths are disabled. The transfer and leaderboard entries are hidden; local play, saves and name editing remain available.

The package retains the game engine and SDK files for TopOn/Thinkup (including ADX/SDM), Tramini, Huawei Ads, Google Ads, Unity Authentication, Cloud Save, Cloud Code, Analytics and PlayFab. Their advertising or online game functions are disabled in this version. Keeping these files does not mean those functions are enabled, and does not mean that the engine creates no local technical records.

Earlier versions included Unity sign-in, cloud time and saves, save transfer, PlayFab rankings and analytics-related code. The old transfer path could also send transfer usernames and passwords as cloud-save data. This does not mean those saves were public. Some earlier requests may already have been received or stored.

Updating does not recall past requests or delete remote records. The operator has not verified every original project’s access settings, storage location or retention period and does not claim direct control over all of those projects. For questions about older records, use the contact below. Opening a policy link or composing an email uses a separate browser or email app and follows that service’s practices.

本版本已移除 Android 互联网访问、网络状态和 Wi-Fi 状态权限，游戏无法直接连接互联网。自动云登录、云时间和存档、存档转移、在线排行榜，以及原服务的分析和启动入口均已关闭。转移与排行榜入口已隐藏；本地游玩、存档和改名继续保留。

安装包仍保留游戏引擎，以及 TopOn/Thinkup（含 ADX/SDM）、Tramini、Huawei Ads、Google Ads、Unity Authentication、Cloud Save、Cloud Code、Analytics 和 PlayFab 的 SDK 文件；它们的广告或联网游戏功能已在本版本停用。保留这些文件不表示相关功能仍已启用，也不表示引擎不会产生本地技术记录。

旧版本包含 Unity 登录、云时间及存档、存档转移、PlayFab 排行榜和分析相关代码。旧转移路径还可能把转移用户名和密码作为云存档数据发送；这不表示那些存档是公开的。旧请求可能已经被接收或保存。

更新不会撤回过去的请求，也不会自动删除远端记录。运营者尚未核实所有原项目的访问设置、保存地域和期限，不声称可以直接管理这些项目。关于旧记录的问题，请使用下方联系方式。打开政策链接或撰写邮件会使用独立的浏览器或邮件应用，并适用该服务的信息处理规则。

Children and families / 儿童与家庭

Users of different ages can play, with the same offline behavior described above. This version does not ask for advertising consent or use an old ad preference as proof of age or parental consent. It adds no age-verification or parental-verification system.

A parent or guardian can contact the operator about a child’s information, including records that may remain from an earlier version. Installing this update does not itself erase those earlier records.

各年龄用户均可游玩，均适用上方说明的相同离线方式。本版本不要求广告同意，也不把旧广告偏好当作年龄或监护人同意的证明；本次没有新增年龄验证或监护人验证系统。

家长或监护人可以联系运营者，咨询孩子的信息，包括旧版本可能留下的记录。安装本次更新本身不会抹除那些历史记录。
